# Task 4: Severity & Case Classification

## Case Classification & Severity Analysis

### Pharmacovigilance Guidelines
- **Serious ADR:** Results in death, life-threatening condition, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly, or other medically important condition.  
- **Expected ADR:** Consistent with known drug information (label or established characteristics).  

### Case Table
| Drug         | Adverse Reaction                          | Seriousness   | Expectedness |
|--------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------|--------------|
| Amoxicillin  | Skin rash, itching                        | Non-serious   | Expected     |
| Ibuprofen    | Gastrointestinal bleeding (hospitalization)| Serious       | Expected     |
| Metformin    | Lactic acidosis                           | Serious       | Unexpected   |
| Atorvastatin | Elevated liver enzymes (mild)             | Non-serious   | Expected     |
| Levofloxacin | Tendon rupture                            | Serious       | Expected     |

### Summary
- **Serious ADRs:** 3  
- **Non-serious ADRs:** 2  
- **Expected ADRs:** 4  
- **Unexpected ADRs:** 1  

---

## Key Learning Points
- **Severity classification** helps prioritize ADRs for urgent intervention.  
- **Expected vs. unexpected ADRs** guide regulatory reporting obligations.  
- **Unexpected serious ADRs** (e.g., Metformin → lactic acidosis) are critical signals for pharmacovigilance.  
- Structured classification ensures **consistent global reporting standards**.
